# Architecture and Product Decisions

Record decisions that future contributors and agents must preserve or consciously revisit.

## Template

### YYYY-MM-DD — Decision title

**Context:** What problem or tradeoff required a decision?

**Decision:** What was chosen?

**Why:** Why was this option selected?

**Consequences:** What becomes easier, harder, or constrained?

**Revisit when:** What future condition should trigger reconsideration?
